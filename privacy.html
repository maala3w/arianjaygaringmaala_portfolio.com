Privacy Policy

This portfolio is a static website and does not collect your information. This page explains how the contact form and other features work.

What is collected

Nothing. This site has no database and does not store anything you type. It does not require an account, and it does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

The contact form only opens your own email app with your message filled in. If you choose to send it, it reaches me like any other email, and I use it only to reply. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.

The portfolio assistant runs entirely in your browser, and your questions are not sent anywhere. Your colour theme choice and whether you have seen the intro are saved in your own browser only.

Third-party resources

The site's hosting provider may keep standard access logs. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and project pages that include charts load the Chart.js library from cdnjs. These providers may receive your IP address as part of delivering those files.

Your choices
