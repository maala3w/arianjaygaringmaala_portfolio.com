    Behind the data.

    A Student. A Researcher at Heart. A Future Data Analyst.

    I am Arian Jay G. Maala, a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student at Biliran Province State University. My academic journey has been shaped by a strong commitment to learning, research, leadership, and continuous improvement.

    I am particularly interested in research because it allows me to investigate problems, examine evidence, analyze information, and develop meaningful conclusions. I believe that data becomes more valuable when it is transformed into insights that help people understand problems and make informed decisions.

    My background in Computer Science has encouraged me to explore the intersection of technology, statistics, data analysis, and machine learning. I am continuously developing my technical skills while seeking opportunities to apply analytical thinking to real-world problems.

    Beyond academics, my involvement in student leadership has strengthened my communication, collaboration, organization, and problem-solving abilities.

    My long-term goal is to become a Data Analyst who can combine research, technical knowledge, and analytical skills to support evidence-based decision-making and contribute to meaningful projects.
